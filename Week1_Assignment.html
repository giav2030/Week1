✨🌈Kassia's Creative Process🌈✨

About her and her artistic process...

Kassia shared their artistic practice in the realm of motion graphics and production design, as well as how she extends it towards physical and other forms of media. Her interests go from cultural elements to be inspired by movies and music videos.

Steps of Kassia's Creative Process
  1. Finding references that inspire her in places like Pinterest, magazines or posts.
  2. Comes up with a story based that flows correctly with the visual aesthetic she created.
  3. Analogue basic sketching and doddling.
  4. Comes up with several digital sketches based on the paper ones.
  5. Takes digital illustrations to Photoshop or Illustrator to start adding color and thinking about composition.
  6. Reaches to peers for feedback before moving forward with the rest of the process.
  7. Once happy with the feedback, she polish and separates the layers. Thinking about each part's motion beforehand, preparing them to get exported to AfterFx.
  8. Export to AfterFx.
  9. Search and add other types of asset Fx like paper or light.
  10. Sets the motion for each of the elements and layers.
  11. Adds sound fx and music.

One of the things she enjoys the most is making the layers move. Spending time in AfterFx is what she considers the best time. She spends several hours to perfectly detail a couple of seconds of animation. She believes that her craft and talent is thanks to hard work, spending lots of time making and learning from their peers and her mentor. She also honed her skills in time management because her work was always tied to tight deadlines.

She has collaborated with producers and writers, stating is another amazing aspect of her work that she enjoys. In her own words:

"I love to collaborate with them to deliver the visual world they imagined to them. I find very satisfying and I believe is a beautiful part of the job. I enjoy meeting producers with a similar aesthetic and thinking process as me. Everything flows better thanks to that."
